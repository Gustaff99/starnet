LEARN BY DOING

Guías paso a paso

One useful outcome at a time. Each guide walks through a task with station diagrams and concrete steps.

First visit? Install StarNet and run your first task before building a conveyor workflow. You can chat with an agent without any belts.

01

Get your crew working

Start with a person and a task, then build your first line.

Configura tu equipoRecruit agents and place the gear that gives them real capabilities.→Construye tu primer flujoPlace a ready-made line and send it a real job.→Work toward a goalTurn goals into milestones and understand quests and XP.→

02

Route work across the station

Understand each piece of a conveyor workflow.

Básicos de cintasFollow a job from its input to its output, and read the floor’s status labels.→Filter & route workSend different kinds of work to different agents.→Split & combine resultsRun several branches, wait for their results, and bring them together.→

03

Make it an ongoing routine

Repeat useful work and reach the station while you’re away.

Crear trabajo repetibleChoose what runs, how it runs, and when it runs.→Configurar autonomíaConfigure unattended work and read the morning report.→Message your stationConnect a messaging channel and send tasks from your phone.→
